# Cover Letter Writing

## Overview
This skill assists jobseekers by drafting professional, tailored cover letters designed to highlight key achievements and align with specific company values and job descriptions.

## Guidelines
- Structure cover letters with a proper professional salutation, strong opening hook, relevant key achievements, company alignment, and a clear call to action.
- Draw context directly from prior discussion turns in the conversation (such as target company, job description, role title, or user background mentioned earlier).
- Maintain a confident, professional, and authentic tone appropriate for the target industry.
